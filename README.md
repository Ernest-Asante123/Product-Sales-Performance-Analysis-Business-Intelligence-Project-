#Product-Sales-Dashboard-PowerBI
**Business Problem**

A retail company was struggling to monitor and evaluate product sales performance across multiple products and time periods. Sales records were stored in raw data tables, making it difficult for management to:

Identify top-performing products.
Compare product sales across different years.
Track monthly sales trends.
Detect products with declining sales.
Make data-driven decisions for inventory and marketing strategies.

As a result, decision-makers spent significant time manually reviewing sales reports, leading to slower business decisions and missed growth opportunities.

**Solution**

To address these challenges, I developed an interactive Power BI Product Sales Dashboard that transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

**The dashboard provides:**

Real-time product sales summaries through KPI cards.
Year-over-year sales comparisons.
Monthly sales trend analysis.
Product performance visualization.
Interactive filtering for deeper analysis.
Product contribution analysis through pie charts.
Dashboard Features
Sales KPI Monitoring

Displays total sales for key products, enabling management to quickly assess performance.

Yearly Sales Analysis

Allows stakeholders to compare product sales across different years and identify growth or decline trends.

Monthly Trend Analysis

Helps management understand seasonal sales patterns and determine peak-performing months.

Product Performance Comparison

Provides side-by-side comparisons of products to identify best-selling and underperforming items.

Interactive Filtering

Users can filter by year to perform focused analysis and make more informed decisions.

**Impact**

The dashboard simplifies sales monitoring and helps decision-makers:

Reduce manual reporting efforts.
Identify top-selling products faster.
Track sales performance trends.
Improve inventory planning.
Support data-driven business decisions.
